Thank you for using Shareholder Benefit Log (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled within the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App (iOS and Android versions) stores the following information entered by the user on your device:

  • Stock ticker codes, company names, purchase dates, share quantities, and acquisition prices
  • Shareholder benefit details, expiration dates, and record dates
  • Optional notes such as long-term holding conditions, the date a benefit was used, and an account holder name (for example, a family member) that you enter yourself
  • Photos of benefit vouchers that you choose to attach (iOS)

This data is stored in your device's local storage (iPhone, iPad, or Android) and is never sent to our servers. The developer does not operate any server and cannot see your data.

iCloud sync (iOS only): If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is enabled for the App, the data above is also saved to your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) so that it can be synced across your iPhone and iPad. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer cannot access it. You can turn sync off at any time in the iOS Settings app (your name > iCloud). The Android version does not use iCloud.

Photos and camera (iOS): You can attach a photo of a benefit voucher by choosing one from your photo library or taking one with the camera. The camera is used only when you tap the button to take a voucher photo. Only the photo you choose or take is saved, inside the App on your device (and in your private iCloud database if iCloud sync is on). The App does not read the rest of your photo library, and photos are never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Calendar and notifications (iOS): When you add an expiration date or last cum-rights date to your calendar, the App opens the standard iOS event screen, and the event is saved only if you confirm it there. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device; no data is sent to a server for them.

2. Third-Party Services

The App communicates with the following third-party services:

  • Yahoo Finance API: Used to fetch stock prices, financial metrics, and shareholder benefit information. Only ticker codes are transmitted.
  • Google AdMob: Used to serve ads. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Google Advertising ID on Android). On iOS, personalized ads require App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.
  • RevenueCat: Used to manage in-app purchases. Only purchase verification is performed; no personal information is collected.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads to free-plan users via Google AdMob. Your device's advertising ID may be used. If you decline ATT permission on iOS, or reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings, non-personalized ads are shown instead.

4. Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App deletes the data stored on that device. If you use iCloud sync on iOS, a copy remains in your iCloud account; to delete it, open the iOS Settings app, tap your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage), select the App, and delete its data. Backup files you export are saved only to the location you choose and are never sent to us.

5. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

This policy may be updated without prior notice. Any changes will be posted on this page.
